WHAT IT IS

Building the combat loop.

Zard is a 3D combat prototype built around responsive melee combat, readable enemy behaviour and timing-driven defensive mechanics. The project evolved from integrating a character combat system into a playable environment into a more complete combat experience with enemies, AI, parry/counter mechanics, stamina and posture.

The goal was not simply to add attacks, but to make the interaction between animation, hit detection, timing, feedback and enemy behaviour feel consistent and deliberate.

MY WORK

Combat implementation

Character & Animation — Character rigging, locomotion and attack animations, with animation blending and transitions refined around combat timing.

Combat Attacks — Implemented Swing Slash, Shield Bash and Poke attacks, plus combo chains and transitions between attacks and abilities.

Hit Detection — Built collision-based combat where attacks register only while the weapon hitbox is active during the strike animation. Player and enemy attacks use the same hit-detection approach for consistent combat rules.

Defensive Mechanics — Added dodge rolls with i-frames, blocking, stamina drain, posture/exhaustion and Block → Parry transitions.

Parry & Counter — Designed a timing-based parry window where raising the shield just before impact staggers the opponent and creates an opening for a counterattack. Animation timing, collider activation and feedback were tuned together.

Enemy AI — Added animated enemies with colliders and pathfinding for patrol/chase behaviour, then introduced a behaviour tree to drive movement, aggression and attacks.

Combat Feedback — Added weapon trails, particle effects, hit reactions and sound effects for swings, footsteps and blocks to strengthen the sense of impact.

SYSTEM BREAKDOWN

How the combat works.

01

Attack → Hitbox → Impact

Weapon hitboxes are activated during the appropriate strike frames rather than treating the whole attack animation as a hit. This keeps attack timing readable and makes collisions feel tied to the animation.

02

Block → Stamina → Posture

Blocking is not a free defensive state. It drains stamina and affects posture; when posture reaches zero, the player becomes exhausted and vulnerable.

03

Parry → Stagger → Counter

The parry is deliberately timing-sensitive. A successful parry staggers the opponent and creates a short counterattack opportunity, giving the mechanic a high-risk, high-reward structure.

04

AI → Behaviour → Combat

Enemies patrol and chase using pathfinding, while a behaviour tree handles movement, aggression and attacks. The AI was still being refined as the prototype evolved.

LEVEL & ATMOSPHERE

From combat arena to world.

Alongside the combat work, I built a Witcher-inspired environment from scratch with a focus on open-world-style immersion and exploration.

  • Game design & level layout — planning terrain structure and placement.
  • Terrain sculpting — shaping ground, valleys and rivers.
  • Object placement — trees, rocks and foliage for environmental detail.
  • Village building — houses, interiors and props.
  • Lighting & atmosphere — day/night settings, post-processing and environmental mood.
  • Third-person controller — a playable controller for exploring the environment.

DESIGN FOCUS

Combat should feel grounded in the world around it.

After refining textures, lighting and ambience, the prototype became a space where the combat systems could be experienced inside a custom-built village rather than in an isolated test scene.
