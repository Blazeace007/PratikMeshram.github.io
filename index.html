ECHO CIRCUIT · CORE SYSTEM

One rule.
Many consequences.

The core mechanic is simple: every 20 seconds, time rewinds. Instead of deleting the player's actions, the game records them as persistent Echoes. The player then plans around their own past behaviour.

02Time rewinds deterministically
